A modern **Next.js & TypeScript** travel platform for visitors exploring Egypt—combining AI-assisted itinerary planning, digital travel permit workflows, verified private guide discovery, and tiered membership benefits.

**Live Demo:** [https://pharaonic-guide.vercel.app/](https://pharaonic-guide.vercel.app/)

---

## Key Features & Architecture

- **AI Trip Planner & Custom Itineraries:** Interactive multi-step itinerary builder generating tailored day-by-day routes across Cairo, Luxor, Aswan, Hurghada, and Siwa.
- **Digital Travel Permits & Private Guides:** Structured permit application workflows and a filterable directory of licensed Egyptologists and private tour specialists.
- **Membership Tiers (`Voyager`, `Premium`, `Heritage`):** Interactive tier comparison matrix with dynamic pricing and concierge perks.
- **Editorial Travel Stories & Guides:** Curated destination guides, cultural etiquette briefings, and traveler reviews.
- **Technical SEO & Performance:** Server-rendered route metadata, Open Graph & Twitter social cards, semantic HTML landmarks, and responsive dark/gold editorial styling.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js, React, TypeScript
- **Styling & UI:** Tailwind CSS, Responsive Editorial Design System, Lucide Icons
- **SEO & Metadata:** Next.js Metadata API, Open Graph, Twitter Cards
- **Deployment:** Vercel
